## Summary
Jokers In Phase is an unofficial fan concept sandbox for joker-deck roguelikes where every joker has a beat: the visitor arranges five jokers over four seeded poker hands, watches a phase ring show who is in sync, and shares the exact line as a link. Select ranked it first of ten (74.5/100; strongest on interestFit, weakest on timeliness). The day went compact (Tier 2) because this machine has 4 CPUs and so only 2 agent slots, and it needed two script workarounds (see Incidents).

## What went wrong (blameless)
* The sanitizer regex shipped as text with seven unicode escapes; a clerk decoded them, the 1-byte size tolerance rejected the helper, both normalize rounds were lost and ideate returned no ideas (about 540k tokens, 29 minutes). Worked around from a scratchpad copy; cards fixed the root cause in kaizen and lab and engineer added guards.
* The spec workflow never returned after its architect agent was interrupted, and the container sat idle for about 2.5 hours of wall clock. Nothing detects a workflow that outlives its phase budget; jester added a playbook row and the idea of a run-file detector is an open question.
* A 4-CPU machine gives only 2 agent slots, so spec and build went compact and the day is Tier 2 with 3804 own lines.
* The first kaizen attempt was refused by one failing test at HEAD that passed on the later full run; the cause is unknown and may be load from the background heartbeat loop.
* The shipped page has one known S2: the scoring cascade and total sit below the fold on every desktop width and far below it on phones.

## Follow-ups for the owner
* Fix `ops/workflows/ideate.js`: the `runFiles` size check cannot survive the unicode escapes in the sanitizer regex (raise the tolerance, or build the regex from char codes). Until then every ideate with a Haiku clerk that decodes escapes returns no ideas.
* Decide what to do on a 4-CPU machine: only 2 agent slots forces every day to Tier 2.
* A workflow that stalls after an interrupted subagent never returns; consider a watchdog in the playbook for a workflow older than its phase cap.
