## What follows (the rules every phase applies)
1. **A real mix.** Each page has one purpose: tool, lesson, explorer, studio or play. Tools and lessons come first; play is welcome but rare. `node scripts/novelty.mjs mix` prints today's plan.
2. **Say what it is, in plain words, first.** Title, tagline and intro name the literal thing ("A calculator that ...", "A lesson on ...", "A game where you drive ..."). A theme or metaphor may decorate; it never replaces the plain description.
3. **Understood in ten seconds, first success in thirty.** One core idea. No prior knowledge needed. At most 8 controls on the first screen, advanced controls behind a "More" toggle. The first frame already shows the thing working (a demo running, a sample input with its result).
4. **Explain concepts for a curious non-expert.** Define every term where it first appears. Worked examples a script has checked. An intro paragraph (what it is, who it is for, what to do first) and an explainer section on every page.
5. **Keep what made day 3 work.** A familiar real-world hook, one clear goal, a warm voice, instant feedback, a first screen that is one picture.
6. **Useful means correct.** A tool gives the right answer, proven against known-answer tests from named sources, and says its limits. A lesson teaches nothing false.
