# Jokers In Phase: DESIGN

**Identity.** A moonlit lab notebook: warm paper page, one always-dark "night sky" scope panel for the phase ring, jokers as flat geometric tokens. No CRT or scanline look, no pixel art, no green felt, no card-back pattern, no mascot, no blue-chips/red-mult pairing (points are amber, mult is magenta). System fonts only. Built on the shell tokens; every colour below is a token on `:root`, flipped under `prefers-color-scheme: dark`.

## Palette (first six become project.json "palette")
Light: `--bg #f3eee4`, `--night #0f1226`, `--accent #5b3fd1`, `--glow #d9f55c`, `--pend #ff9f6e`, `--gold #e0a82e`, `--surface #fffdf8`, `--fg #1d1b26`, `--muted #5a5866`, `--border #d9d0bf`, `--accent-fg #ffffff`, `--pts #9a5b0b`, `--mult #a3225f`, `--ok #1c7a4a`, `--warn #9c3b00`.
Dark: `--bg #0d0f1a`, `--surface #161a2b`, `--fg #ecebf6`, `--muted #a8a6ba`, `--border #2b3050`, `--accent #a995ff`, `--accent-fg #11131a`, `--pts #f0b84a`, `--mult #f27cb4`, `--ok #5fd39a`, `--warn #ff9a62`.
Night panel (both schemes): `--night #0f1226`, `--night-2 #181c3a`, `--night-fg #e8e9f6`, `--night-muted #aab0d8`, `--night-line #5560a8` (ring track, 3.2:1), `--glow #d9f55c` (Fireflies), `--pend #ff9f6e` (Pendulum), `--bridge #ffe8a3` (Handshake link), `--silver #c3c8d6`. Card faces stay white in both schemes; red suits `#b3261e`. All text pairs checked at 4.5:1 or better.

## Type, space, shape
System stack from the shell. Scale: 0.8125rem labels, 0.9375rem body-small, 1rem body, 1.25rem panel heads, h1 from the shell, score `clamp(2rem,1.4rem+3vw,3.2rem)` tabular-nums, steps in the mono stack. Spacing 4/8/12/16/24/32/48. Radius: card 8, token 12, button 10, panel 14 (shell `--radius`), pill 999. 1px `--border`; shell shadows.

## Components
Fan note; challenge nav (segmented, 44 px); coach line; hand tile (five mini cards, hand name, base, score; states played, next, waiting); slot token 56x76 (glyph, name; states default, hover, focus-visible, grabbed, changed, hint, disabled); rail and bench rows; action bar; score panel (hand, total, par bar); scope panel (ring, sync meter, phase table); steps panel (tabs "Last hand" and "If you play now", ordered list); result panel; concept card (SVG; par, silver, gold); sandbox controls; notice; fatal; About.

## Layout
- **360:** one column: header, h1, tagline, fan note, challenge nav (2x2), coach, deal (2x2 tiles), rail (five 56 px slots on one row), bench, actions (Play full width, three quiet buttons below), score, scope (ring fills the width up to 320 px), phase table, steps, result, card, sandbox, About.
- **768:** two columns 1.1fr / 0.9fr. Left: deal, rail, bench, actions, score, steps. Right (sticky): scope, result, card. Nav in one row.
- **1280:** max width 1160, columns 7/5, ring 340 px, deal in one row of four tiles. Above the fold: title block, deal, rail, bench, Play on the left; scope and sync on the right.

## Motion (WAAPI plus CSS; hidden tab stops everything)
- Cascade: each step 360 ms, `cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)`, 8 px slide and fade; the slot gets a 240 ms outline pulse; running totals count up in 240 ms; the final score lands 1 to 1.06 to 1 in 280 ms. Any new state or key press completes it instantly.
- Ring: dots tween 520 ms ease-in-out between discrete states; a flash pulses the radius 320 ms. Slot swap: FLIP 180 ms ease-out.
- Lock: one glow of 900 ms ease-in-out on `#scope` (overlay alpha at most 0.12, under 10% luminance change), never twice within 4 s; silver 600 ms at 0.08. Together the Fireflies blink fewer than 2 times a second.
- Reduced motion (CSS and `matchMedia`): cascade rows appear at once, the ring is drawn once with no tween or pulse (the phase table is the primary read), no glow (a static "Locked" badge and border colour instead), no FLIP, no count-up.

## Art and sound
Inline SVG only, 48x48 viewBox, flat fills, `currentColor` strokes 1.75. Glyphs: Steady hexagon with a bar and plus; Twofold two overlapping circles; Tally four strokes with a slash in a rounded square; Pendulum pivot, arm and bob; Handshake two clasped arcs; Lantern circle in a ring; Ember diamond with an inner glow; Spark eight-point star. Family top bar: add amber, multiply magenta, points amber, phased lime or coral. Suits are four SVG paths; ranks are text. Ring: phase 0 at 12 o'clock, clockwise, 64 ticks, majors at 0, 16, 32, 48; Fireflies are circle, diamond, star dots with an L, E, S letter; benched dots at 40% with an outline; Pendulum is a coral dot at the top or bottom with a faint arc; the Handshake bridge is a dashed `--bridge` line between its neighbours' dots (solid when matched).
Sound: muted by default, `#btn-sound` toggles (saved in `jip.v1.ui`); AudioContext created on the first gesture inside try/catch; cascade tick sine 660 Hz plus 40 Hz per step (45 ms, gain 0.04), flash blip 1320 Hz (90 ms), lock chord A4 plus E5 (700 ms, gain 0.05).

## Voice and microcopy (dry, friendly, nerdy; no wager words)
- **Fixed lines.** Tagline: "Five jokers, one beat. Swap them into step, lock three fireflies in phase, and beat par." Fan note: "Unofficial fan concept in the style of joker-deck roguelikes such as Balatro. Not affiliated with or endorsed by its developer or publisher. No game art, fonts, logos or audio are used; numbers and art are invented." Under the score: "Points are only a score: nothing here costs or pays anything." Keys line: "Keys: P play, U undo, R retry, H hint, M sound, 1 to 3 challenges, S sandbox."
- **Challenges.** 1 "First Beat": "Coupling off. Fireflies blink alone. Fix the order and reach par." 2 "Soft Pull": "Neighbours nudge each other's beat, nearest first. Lock the fireflies for silver or gold." 3 "Shake On It": "Medium coupling, and a Handshake that scores only when its neighbours match." Sandbox: "Free deal: no cards, but the exact best score is still shown." Coupling Off, Soft, Medium, Firm with flash bonus +6, +5, +4, +3.
- **Buttons.** "Play hand {n}" (finished: "Round over"), "Undo swap", "Retry round", "Hint", "Sound on" and "Sound off", "Copy link" (then "Link copied", or "Press Ctrl+C" when the clipboard is blocked), "Deal", "New seed", "Reload". Labels: "Deal number (1 to 99999)", "Coupling", "Sync" with "{pct}%" or a dash and "locks at 92", tabs "Last hand" and "If you play now".
- **Jokers and hands.** Steady "Adds +4 mult."; Twofold "Multiplies mult by 2."; Tally "Adds +12 points."; Pendulum "Swings each hand: out adds +8 mult, back multiplies mult by 2."; Handshake "Multiplies mult by 3 when the jokers on both sides are in matching phase."; Lantern, Ember, Spark "Firefly, slow, middle or quick beat. Flashes when its phase passes the top: adds mult, more when others flash together." Hand names: High card, Pair, Two pair, Three of a kind, Straight, Flush, Full house, Four of a kind, Straight flush.
- **Steps.** "{Hand}: {pts} × {mult}"; "Steady adds +4 mult"; "Twofold doubles mult"; "Tally adds +12 points"; "Pendulum swings out: +8 mult" and "swings back: mult ×2"; "Handshake: neighbours match, mult ×3", "neighbours are out of phase", "both neighbours need a phase", "needs a neighbour on each side"; "{Name} flashes: +{value} mult" plus " ({n} together)" when n is 2 or more; "{Name} is dark this beat"; "= {score}".
- **Coach** (first match wins). Finished: gold "All three fireflies locked. The whole rail breathes together."; silver "Two fireflies locked. One more for gold."; par "Par reached with {n} to spare."; below "{n} short of par. A retry costs nothing." Challenge 1, hand 1, no swap: "Hand 1 played itself: points × mult, joker by joker, left to right. Order matters. Tap one joker, then another, to swap them and watch 'If you play now'." Pending swap: "Swap made. Compare 'If you play now', undo it, or play hand {n}." Challenge 2 start: "Fireflies pull toward their neighbours. Watch the sync meter: 92 or more is a lock." Challenge 3 start: "The Handshake multiplies by 3 only when both neighbours are in matching phase." Default: "Hand {n} of 4. One swap per beat, then play."
- **Results and states.** "Best possible on this deal: {best} (all 17,576 lines checked)."; "No lock this round."; loading "Dealing the cards"; empty card "Practice sketch. Finish the round to earn this card."; hint "Try swapping slot {a} with {b}." and "Hold still: no swap is best before this hand."; fatal "Something jammed the machinery. Reload to deal again."
- **Notices.** swap-limit "One swap per beat. Undo it or play the hand."; bad-hash "That link did not parse, so here is a fresh deal."; seed-clamped "Deal numbers run from 1 to 99999."; line-clipped "That link had more moves than hands; the extra moves were ignored."; no-hint "Hints unlock after three rounds below par."
- **Card and share.** Card: "Concept card", "Deal #{seed}", "Coupling {label}", "Rail, scored left to right", tier ribbon "Gold lock", "Silver lock", "Par card" or "Practice sketch". Share text: "Jokers In Phase · {challenge name} · {total} points · {tier}" plus the link.
